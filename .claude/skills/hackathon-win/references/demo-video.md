# The demo video

The single highest-leverage artifact in a hackathon submission. Most judges watch it; many never open the live site. Habitat Pulse skipped it and did not place.

Check the rules for a length cap first — many hackathons hard-cap at 2 or 3 minutes and **disqualify** or stop watching past it.

## Target: 90 seconds

| Time | Content |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | The wow moment. No logo, no title card, no "hi my name is." Start mid-action, on the thing. |
| 0:10–0:25 | The problem, one sentence, spoken plainly. Why it matters, one sentence. |
| 0:25–1:10 | The demo. Real, live, unedited-looking. One continuous path a real person would take. |
| 1:10–1:25 | How it works — 15 seconds, plain language, no architecture diagram. |
| 1:25–1:30 | What's next, and one honest limitation. |

Ruthless rule: **if a second doesn't earn its place, cut it.** A tight 70 seconds beats a padded 3 minutes.

## Non-negotiables

- **Narration.** A silent screen recording with background music scores badly. A human voice explaining what's happening is worth more than any edit.
- **Real data.** Show the actual API responding, the actual device, the actual file. Never a hardcoded demo path.
- **Captions/subtitles.** Judges watch muted, on laptops, in noisy rooms.
- **Public and playable.** Unlisted YouTube is fine. Private, login-walled, or "request access" is a zero.
- **Embedded on the submission page**, not just linked.

## Common failures

- Opening on the landing page or login screen — 10 seconds of nothing
- Screen-recording a walkthrough of every menu
- Explaining the tech stack before showing what it does
- No audio
- Over 3 minutes
- Showing a mockup and describing it as if it works — judges notice, and it costs more than the missing feature would have
- Recording once and shipping the first take

## Production, cheap and fast

Record with QuickTime (macOS: File → New Screen Recording) or OBS. Do a scripted dry run first — write the 90 seconds out as actual sentences and read them. Three takes, keep the best. Trim in iMovie or CapCut. Upload unlisted to YouTube.

Budget ~20% of remaining project time for this. It is not the last thing you do with leftover minutes; it is a planned deliverable with its own slot.
